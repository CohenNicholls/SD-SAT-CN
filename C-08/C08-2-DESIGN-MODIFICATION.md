# 4. Explain the Causal Chain

Complete the following in project-specific language.

**Test result / feedback:**

> I found...

**Design problem:**

> This showed that...

**Modification:**

> Therefore, I changed...

**Improvement:**

> This improves the solution because...

Now combine the four parts into one clear explanation you could give in the viva:

> I found ______________________________ during testing/feedback. This showed ______________________________. Therefore I changed ______________________________ to ______________________________. This improves the solution because ______________________________.

Do not memorise the sentence word-for-word. You should understand the reasoning well enough to explain it naturally.

# 7. Viva Practice

You will have approximately **8 minutes** with your teacher.

Be ready to answer these questions using your own prepared evidence:

1. Which part of your design needed changing?
2. How did you know it needed changing?
3. Show me the before and after. What changed?
4. What specific test result or feedback drove this modification?
5. Why is the modified design better?
6. Did your evaluation criteria change? Why/why not?
7. What contingency have you planned?
8. What would you do if that issue occurred?

The strongest answers connect to **specific evidence**, not generic explanations.

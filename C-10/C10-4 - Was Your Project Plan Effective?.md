# **Three Biggest Changes** ###
## **Change 1 - Testing was delayed** ##

**Why it happened:**
Development and refinement took longer than originally expected, so beta testing occurred later than the original Gantt schedule.

**Effect on the project:**
This delayed completion but gave me an opportunity to test the finished software with real users and identify problems.

**Effect:**
Helpful for software quality, but negative for the overall timeline.

## **Change 2 - The final project extended into September**

**Why it happened:**
The original Gantt chart planned the project to finish in August, but beta testing and evaluation continued into September.

**Effect on the project:**
The project took longer than originally planned, but I was able to collect more detailed evidence before completing the evaluation.

**Effect:**
The delay had a negative effect on the schedule but a useful effect on the quality of the evaluation.

## **Change 3 - More refinement was needed after development**

**Why it happened:**
Beta testers identified problems that were not obvious during development. These included login complexity, unclear input requirements, button visibility and filtering usability.

**Effect on the project:**
The additional feedback showed which parts of the software needed improvement and helped create specific recommendations for future changes.

**Effect:**
Helpful because it gave clear evidence for future improvements, although it increased the amount of work required.

## **Biggest Gap Between the Original Gantt and Actual Project**

The biggest gap was the timing of testing and final evaluation.

The original Gantt chart planned testing for July and the final evaluation for August. In reality, beta testing continued into September. This shows that the original timeline underestimated the amount of time required to finish, test and evaluate the software.

## **Was My Project Plan Effective Overall?**

My project plan was partly effective.

The Gantt chart provided a clear sequence of stages and helped organise the project from requirements and design through to development, testing and evaluation. The major stages of the project were correctly identified.

However, the timeline was not completely accurate because the project continued beyond the planned August completion date. Testing occurred later than planned, and the final evaluation therefore also moved later.

The beta testing results show that the additional time was useful because it identified specific issues with login, input requirements, buttons and filtering. Therefore, the plan was effective for organising the overall project structure, but the time estimates could have been more realistic.

## **What I Would Plan Differently Next Time**

Next time I would allow more time for development and beta testing and include extra time for unexpected problems. I would also leave a dedicated period between beta testing and final evaluation so that feedback can be used to make improvements before the project is submitted.
